# Re-issue of every bundle, 2026-11-24

Every bundle in this repository that has an upstream commit, re-audited at its recorded commit, tree, language,
risk profile and analyst decisions with QSI engine 1.20.0 (5 bundles with 1.20.1, which the comparison prompted: govulncheck's coverage line for Go modules that hold only tests or samples, and its retry with cgo), on 2026-11-24. Before is the bundle as published at
commit `7035b9a` of this repository (`git show 7035b9a:<path>/report.json`), the re-issue of 22 November with engine
1.16.2; after is the bundle published now. Every figure below is read from those two files. `audits/nitro` and
`audits/lottery-demo` keep their bundles (see the README). Why the figures moved is in the README's section of the
same name; each bundle's own report states its engine, its coverage and what it set aside.
`studies/institutional/arc-node` and `studies/l1/arc-node` are one bundle, published in both studies.

*Advisories* counts the findings about a pinned dependency named in an OSV.dev advisory; *unlikely to apply* is
how many of them the engine judged so, each with its reason in the finding: no call path from the product's
packages to the affected functions (govulncheck, for Go), a module only tests or tools use, affected functions
the code does not name, a Cargo feature that is off, or a platform the build does not target. *Called* is how
many govulncheck traced to a call; *key storage* counts the findings of the key-storage rules (`KEYMGMT-*`).
