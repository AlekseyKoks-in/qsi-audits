Methodology

The audit was performed by the QSI engine 1.16.2. Each analyzer emits facts (rule, parameters, evidence); finding texts are generated from the rule catalog. Methodology sources are listed under "Sources and normative documents".

Prioritization uses an explainable weighted model (severity, exploitability, exposure, confidentiality/integrity/availability impact weighted by the "Web3 project" profile, asset criticality, urgency, ease of remediation) with a confidence multiplier. CVSS v3.1 is applied only to technical vulnerabilities with a defined vector. Long-term quantum risks use Mosca's inequality; their severity is capped at Medium and they are never presented as exploitable vulnerabilities.

Finding classes: confirmed technical vulnerability; known vulnerable component (a dependency version named in an advisory; whether the affected code is reachable is assessed separately, and a component confirmed by review becomes a vulnerability); confirmed configuration error; potential cryptographic risk; long-term post-quantum risk; improvement recommendation; insufficient data. Heuristic findings are marked preliminary; analyst decisions are shown next to the findings.
