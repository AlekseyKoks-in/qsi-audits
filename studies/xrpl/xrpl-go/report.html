Methodology

Prioritization uses an explainable weighted model (severity, exploitability, exposure, confidentiality/integrity/availability impact weighted by the "Web3 project" profile, asset criticality, urgency, ease of remediation) with a confidence multiplier. CVSS v3.1 is applied only to technical vulnerabilities with a defined vector. Long-term quantum risks use Mosca's inequality; their severity is capped at Medium and they are never presented as exploitable vulnerabilities.
